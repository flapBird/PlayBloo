Why we're featuring Incredig

Incredig scratches the classic incremental-mining itch: a simple dig loop that quietly snowballs into an automated operation. It is a prototype, so don't expect hours of content yet, but the core loop already feels good — and the "something strange below" hook is reason enough to keep the drill spinning.

About Incredig

Incredig is a short sci-fi incremental mining game from ThinKING Cat Game about digging far too deep. You start with a single drill, break rock pixel by pixel, and collect the ore that comes tumbling down.
